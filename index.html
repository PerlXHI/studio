Monetization & Content ID

Target policy: monetized content on YouTube, Twitch, and TikTok is permitted under the applicable license; Perivelle works are not registered with third-party Content ID systems. Publish this claim only after all samples, collaborators, distribution, and rights administration have been cleared.

File specifications & deliverables

24-bit WAV masters; BGM exported as seamless loops; original songs and covers may include off-vocal or instrumental versions; stems are available as an add-on or according to the package. Compressed MP3 previews are provided for streaming.

Revisions & Rush Orders

Includes two minor revisions after demo approval. Major concept changes, additional stems, rush orders, and out-of-scope revisions may incur extra fees agreed in advance.
